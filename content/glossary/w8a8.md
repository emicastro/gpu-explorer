name: "W8A8 and W8A16"
short: "W is the weight bit width and A the activation bit width. W8A16 stores weights in FP8 and multiplies in BF16; W8A8 also multiplies in FP8."
related: ["fp8-scaling", "tensor-core"]
sources: []
---

W8A16 saves bytes (the weights are half the size) but not FLOPs, because the multiplication still runs in BF16. W8A8 saves bytes and doubles the peak compute, because the [[tensor-core|tensor cores]] run FP8 at twice the BF16 rate. See [[fp8-scaling]] for how FP8 values are scaled.
