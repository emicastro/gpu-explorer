The [[l2-cache]] is a large on-chip cache in the middle of the [[die]], in two halves. Every [[sm]] can reach all of it. Every read from or write to [[hbm]] passes through it.

## Why it exists

[[hbm]] is far away and its bandwidth is limited. When several SMs need the same bytes, the L2 lets only the first request go out to memory, and the others are served on the chip.

## In a decode step

Every byte that reaches an [[sm]] from [[hbm]] passes through here, so a kernel's traffic is the same at both. The weights are far too big to stay in the L2 between steps, so each step streams them through once. The L2 saves trips within a kernel: the small activation tensor read by every block, and, with [[gqa]], a KV head that several query heads read.

## Think of it as

The pantry shared by every station in the kitchen. If one cook already fetched something from the storeroom, the next one finds it on the pantry shelf.
