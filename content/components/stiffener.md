The stiffener is the metal ring around the edge of the package, surrounding the interposer and its chips.

## Why it exists

The package heats up under load and cools when idle, and its silicon, copper and organic layers grow and shrink by different amounts. Without a frame the substrate would bow, straining the tiny solder joints under the chips until they crack. The ring holds it flat.

## In a decode step

Nothing that the model sees. It only keeps the package in shape while the step heats it.

## Think of it as

The frame around a stretched canvas: it does not paint anything, but without it the canvas would warp.
